## Map and motion

HTML owns labels and buttons. An SVG shows connectors without WebGL. Geometry
comes from rendered label-group bounds; opaque groups protect all captions.
Keep the spatial map at narrow widths; a list requires an explicit action.
Three.js adds dimensional traces and brief flow motion along the selected
project's connection to delivery governance. No repeated sculpture. Load the
renderer only when visible and permitted, stop rendering when motion settles,
dispose offscreen, and honor reduced motion, data saving and WebGL failure.
