# SLZ brand assets

Generated with OpenAI Image Generation on September 13, 2026 for the owner's
authorized signature variant. These are public brand drawings, not a scan or
trace of the owner's handwritten signature. Keep the original upload out of
the repository.

- `slz-signature.png`: horizontal SLZ, open S, tall forward L, angular Z and a
  short finish. Used above the printed name in the footer. Next Image serves
  appropriately sized versions; CSS blends the white ground into either theme.
- `slz-symbol.png`: a separate compact, bold SLZ with open counters, drawn in
  the same family for small sizes. `app/icon.tsx` renders a 64px PNG at build
  time, and the social preview embeds the same source.

Direction: confident handwritten black strokes on flat white, no frame,
circle, sun, ornament or long trailing line. The favicon must preserve all
three initials rather than squeezing the horizontal signature into a square.
The Sol de Mayo and anthem remain a separate cultural element in the footer.
